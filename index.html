ABOUT DECCAN SAFAR

1. Who We Are

Deccan Safar is an India-focused travel and tourism platform created to help people discover the beauty, culture, heritage, and diversity of India. From the snow-covered mountains of Kashmir to the peaceful backwaters of Kerala, we bring amazing destinations from across the country together in one place.

2. Our Mission

Our mission is to make travelling easier, more exciting, and accessible for everyone by providing useful information about popular destinations, tourist attractions, local cultures, historical places, and unique travel experiences. We aim to inspire people to explore every corner of India.

3. Why Choose Deccan Safar?

Deccan Safar offers a simple and convenient way to explore India's most beautiful destinations. We provide information about famous tourist spots, adventure activities, local food, cultural experiences, and hidden gems. Whether you love mountains, beaches, historical monuments, or nature, we help you discover the perfect destination for your next journey.

4. Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted travel companion for every explorer by connecting people with India's incredible destinations, rich traditions, and unforgettable experiences. We aim to promote Indian tourism and inspire everyone to travel, explore, and create beautiful memories.

Mumbai

City of Dreams
  • State: Maharashtra, India
  • Famous for: Bollywood, Marine Drive, Gateway of India & street food
  • Best time to visit: October - February
  • Travel duration: 3-5 days
  • Popular attractions: Gateway of India, Juhu Beach, Elephanta Caves & Marine Drive

Jammu & Kashmir

Paradise on Earth
  • State: Jammu & Kashmir, India
  • Famous for: Breathtaking mountains, Dal Lake, snow-capped peaks & serene beauty
  • Best time to visit: April - October (Snow lovers: December - February)
  • Travel duration: 5-7 days
  • Popular attractions: Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, Shankaracharya Temple, Betaab Valley

Himachal Pradesh

Abode of the Himalayas
  • State: Himachal Pradesh, India
  • Famous for: Snowy Mountains, Adventure Sports & Scenic Beauty
  • Best time to visit: March - June, September - November
  • Travel duration: 5-7 days
  • Popular attractions: Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Kullu, Spiti Valley

Kerala

God's Own Country
  • State: Kerala, India
  • Famous for: Backwaters, Beaches, Ayurveda, Spices & Lush Greenery
  • Best time to visit: September - March
  • Travel duration: 5-7 days
  • Popular attractions: Alleppey Backwaters, Munnar, Kovalam Beach, Thekkady, Wayanad
